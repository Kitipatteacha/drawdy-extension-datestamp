# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Stamps look like stickers: a coloured badge in a white rim with an icon and
  a two-tone date, tilted or straight. Each stamp is now one image.
- Pick the status (now an icon), colour, format and language from a bar
  above the selected stamps.
- New colours, including a metallic Gold and a Rainbow, and new formats, such
  as the weekday alone, the month or the quarter. Dates can be written in
  eight languages.
- The panel is a month calendar with Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow, and
  arrow-key and Page Up/Down navigation.
- New stamps take the look of the last one. ⌘↵ adds a stamp.
- A new icon: a little tilted stamp.
- Stamps from 0.1.0 are redrawn as the new kind on their first change.

## 0.1.0

- First release: stamp a status and a date on the board, with 5 statuses and
  7 date formats, starting from today.
- Select a stamp to get a bar above it: change its status, set it to today, or
  switch its date format in one click. Pick any other date in the panel.
- Edit several stamps at once by selecting them together.
